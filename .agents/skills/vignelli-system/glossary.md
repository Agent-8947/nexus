# Glossary: Vignelli Design Language

**Appropriateness** — The search for the right solution, avoiding the merely fashionable.
**Design is One** — The philosophy that the same principles apply to all design scales.
**Discipline** — Intellectual rigor required to maintain quality and consistency.
**Intellectual Elegance** — The state of design that is simple, clear, and profound.
**Modernism** — The movement emphasizing functionality, clarity, and the removal of ornament.
**Modular Grid** — A structural framework of horizontal and vertical divisions.
**Pragmatics** — The practical effectiveness and understandability of a design.
**Semantics** — The study of the meaning and history behind a design subject.
**Syntactics** — The formal relationship between design elements (type, grid, etc.).
**Timelessness** — Design that remains relevant across decades by avoiding trends.
